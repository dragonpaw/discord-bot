# Refresh the SubDay weekly prompts

All four prompts for each of the 52 weeks of "Where I am Led"
(`dragonpaw_bot/plugins/subday/weeks/week_NN.md`), plus the shared instructions
(`weeks/rules.md`), consolidated for review on 2026-10-26. Each prompt shows the
**current** text and either a **proposed** rewrite or *keep as-is*.

## Why

The source material is old. It assumes a pre-internet world (dictionaries,
"the safe anonymity of your computer", Santa's workshop), leans heavily on
metaphor and poetic aphorism, and assumes the reader already has an Owner (and
that the Owner is "he"). That's hard going for a lot of readers today, and
especially for autistic participants, who may not be able to tell what a
metaphor is standing in for, or what they're supposed to *write* in response
to a statement.

A handful of prompts also give advice that cuts against what we now know
about neurodivergence and consent: "be low maintenance and minimize
complaints", "keep your mouth closed", "read your Owner's facial expressions",
"act as if", and one that describes the subconscious overriding a conscious
"no". Those get rewritten rather than just clarified.

## How the rewrites work

- **Fix the instructions once.** Guideposts and Thoughts are statements, not
  questions, so readers can't always tell what to write about them. Rather than
  turning all 156 of them into questions, the rewritten `rules.md` below says
  plainly what a response can look like. Most of the individual prompts can
  then stay as they are.
- **Quotes from famous people stay, with an "In other words:" line added.**
  This keeps the original wording and also says plainly what it means.
  Misspelled attributions are fixed. Two quotes are replaced outright (weeks 12
  and 13; see below).
- **Short poetic one-liners get the same treatment:** keep the line, add
  "In other words:".
- **Longer passages that are metaphorical, dated, or harmful get rewritten.**
- **Owners are "they", not "he".** Use "Owner" with a capital O throughout.
- **Typos are fixed** wherever they appear, even in prompts marked
  *keep as-is*.

To apply: edit each `week_NN.md` and `rules.md`, then delete this file.

## Instructions (`rules.md`)

**Current:**

> 1. Below you'll find a 'Guidepost', two 'Thoughts for the week' and a 'Writing assignment'. Please write out your thoughts for the first three prompts, and then do the writing assignment. Usually the first three are only a few sentences each, whereas the final assignment will be a bit longer. (Take your time, reflect, and write when you're ready.)
> 2. Show your work to your Owner. Or if you are not Owned, then check in with any member of staff and discuss your four answers with them.

**Proposed:**

> 1. Each week you get four prompts: a **Guidepost**, two **Thoughts**, and a **Writing assignment**.
>    - **Guidepost and Thoughts:** write a few sentences for each. There's no right answer. You can say what it means to you, whether you agree or disagree (disagreeing is completely fine), or give an example from your own life.
>    - **Writing assignment:** this one's longer. A few paragraphs is plenty.
>    - **No Owner right now?** Answer based on a past dynamic, the one you'd like to have, or how the idea applies to you in general.
>    - Write wherever works for you: pen and paper, a Google Doc, a notes app on your phone, anything. Bullet points are fine. Take your time and write when you're ready.
> 2. Show your work to your Owner. If you aren't Owned, share it with any staff member and talk through your answers together.

*Why:* readers no longer have to guess what "write out your thoughts" on a
statement means, and participants without an Owner are covered once here
instead of in every prompt.
