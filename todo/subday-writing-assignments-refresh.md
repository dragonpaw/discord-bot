# Refresh the SubDay writing assignments

All 52 weekly writing assignments from `dragonpaw_bot/plugins/subday/weeks/week_NN.md`,
consolidated for review (2026-10-26). Each week shows the **current** text and,
where it seemed worth changing, a **proposed** rewrite. Weeks marked *keep as-is*
read fine already.

Why: the source material is old. It assumes a pre-internet world (dictionaries,
printed books vs. film, Santa's workshop), leans on metaphor and open-ended
"be creative" prompts that are hard to decode if you're autistic, and often
assumes the participant already has an Owner. The rewrites aim to:

- Say literally what's being asked. If a prompt uses a metaphor, say what the
  metaphor is standing in for.
- Give a starting structure (a few guiding questions or examples) instead of a
  blank page, while keeping it optional.
- Allow for participants without a current Owner.
- Count online life as real life: friends, creators, fandoms, games, fanfic.
- Offer a way out of prompts that can land badly (childhood, school, body
  image) without making a big deal of it.

Only the Writing Assignment sections are covered here; the Guidepost and Thought
sections of each week are untouched. To apply, edit the `## Writing Assignment`
section of each `week_NN.md` and delete this file.

## Week 1 — Role Models

**Current:** Name three fictional characters who would be good role models for an aspiring slave and explain why.

**Proposed:** Pick three fictional characters — from books, shows, anime, games, movies, fanfic, anything — who you think would be good role models for a slave. For each one, say what they do or what they're like that you'd want to copy.

## Week 2 — Most Valuable Asset

**Current:** What do you feel is the most valuable asset you have to offer your Owner? Explain why.

**Proposed:** What's the best thing you bring to your Owner (or would bring to a future Owner)? It can be a skill, a personality trait, something you do for them — anything. Why do you think it matters most?

## Week 3 — Overcoming a Limit

**Current:** Name one limit that you have consensually overcome. Why was it a limit and what enabled you to overcome it?

**Proposed:** A limit is something you used to say no to. Name one limit you've moved past, by your own choice. Why was it a limit in the first place? What changed that made it okay for you?

If you haven't moved past any limits (which is completely fine), write about a limit you have and why it's important to you.

## Week 4 — Indirect Service

**Current:** Indirect service is something that benefits or pleases your Owner that does not include interacting directly with your Owner, list ten ways that you can indirectly serve your Owner

**Proposed:** Indirect service means doing something for your Owner when they aren't there or you aren't talking to them — like tidying a shared space, prepping their coffee, sorting their calendar, or keeping a promise they'll never check on. List ten ways you could serve your Owner (or a future Owner) like this.

## Week 5 — Emotional Fulfillment

**Current:** Name three things that you need in order to be emotionally fulfilled by your service and explain why.

**Proposed:** What three things do you need for serving to actually feel good to you? (Examples: praise, routine, knowing exactly what's expected, physical affection, feeling noticed.) For each one, explain why you need it.

## Week 6 — Entertainment

**Current:** Entertainment is a form of service. Describe a time when you provided entertainment for the person you were serving.

**Proposed:** Making someone laugh or have a good time counts as service. Describe a time you did that for someone you were serving — a joke, a game you played together, a performance, a meme you sent, anything. If you haven't served anyone yet, describe a time you entertained a friend or partner.

## Week 10 — Reality Check

**Current:** The reality of being a slave is very different than the fantasy. What are some of the things about being a slave that are different from what you imagined they would be.

**Proposed:** Being a slave in real life is usually different from how it looks in fantasy, porn, or fiction. What has turned out differently from what you expected? If you haven't been in a dynamic yet, what have you learned from others that surprised you?

## Week 11 — A Song in Your Heart

**Current:** Name one song that you would sing to your Owner that would describe your relationship and explain why you have chosen that song. (If you are not in a relationship, what song describes how you would like to feel about your Owner.)

**Proposed:** Pick one song that describes your relationship with your Owner, and explain which parts of it fit and why. Lyrics, mood, or just the vibe all count. (No Owner right now? Pick a song for how you'd want to feel about one.)

## Week 12 — What Makes You Work Well

**Current:** In what practical ways do your specific tendencies, defined limits, and need for clear communication actively enhance the stability and daily function of your dynamic with your Owner?

**Proposed:** Think about the things that are specific to you: your habits, your limits, how you need things communicated. How do those things actually help your dynamic run smoothly day to day? Give real examples if you can.
