## Guidepost: Responsibility

I will strive to acknowledge the consequences of my actions. I will accept praise when it is merited and I will make amends when I falter.

## Thought 1

When my Owner isn't around, it's still my job to look after myself — eating, sleeping, taking my meds, staying safe. I belong to them, and I should take as much care of myself as I would of anything else they value.

## Thought 2

You have blindfolded me so that I can see more clearly, you have silenced me so that I can speak more eloquently, and you have bound me so that I can have true freedom.

*In other words: when my Owner takes away a sense or a freedom, it can help me focus and feel more present, not less.*
