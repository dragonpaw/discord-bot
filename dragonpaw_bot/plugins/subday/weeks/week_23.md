## Guidepost: Joy

Never underestimate the value of laughter. Where there is laughter, there is joy. Where there is joy, there is no room for anger.

## Thought 1

Make sure you do not place burdens on your Owner that they have not consented to accept. For instance, if you retain control of your finances, then be prepared to accept the responsibility for your financial success or failure.

## Thought 2

When I am allowed to see the passionate emotions of my Owner, I am thrilled by the intimacy and humbled by the trust that the intimacy demonstrates.

## Writing Assignment: A Song in My Heart — Part Two

What song would you most like your Owner (or a future Owner) to sing or dedicate to you? What makes that song special to you?
