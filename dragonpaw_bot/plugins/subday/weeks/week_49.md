## Guidepost: Sacrifice

For anything worth having, one must pay the price; and the price is always work, patience, love, self-sacrifice — no paper currency, no promises to pay, but the gold of real service. — John Burroughs

*In other words: anything worth having costs real effort — work, patience, love, and giving things up. You can't get it with promises; only actually doing the work counts.*

## Thought 1

How do you know when you've become truly Owned in your heart? It's different for everyone. For me, it was realizing I wanted to do everything I could to meet my Owner's needs and wants, including the ones that didn't come naturally to me. (That never means overriding a real "no". Your limits and safewords still count.)

## Thought 2

Submission is a journey, and the only way to buy a ticket for the trip is to trust.
