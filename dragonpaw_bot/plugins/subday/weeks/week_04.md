## Guidepost: Courtesy

Being polite and considerate makes things go more smoothly with people. A few seconds of courtesy now can save hours of fixing hurt feelings later.

## Thought 1

Loyalty is more than only submitting to your Owner. It also means assuming your Owner meant well when something is unclear, and taking worries or complaints to your Owner first, before venting to other people. The exception is if you ever feel unsafe: reach out to a friend or staff right away. That isn't disloyal.

## Thought 2

Pushing your limits can be a way to show trust, but only go as far and as fast as you're actually ready for. Be honest with yourself about that. Some limits will stay limits forever, and that's fine. Others might change as you come to trust your Owner more. You'll know you're ready when the thing no longer feels scary.

## Writing Assignment: Indirect Service

Indirect service means doing something for your Owner when they aren't there or you aren't talking to them. List ten ways you could serve your Owner (or a future Owner) like this.
