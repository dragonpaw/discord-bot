## Guidepost: Stillness

Your real feelings are easy to miss when life is loud and busy. Take some quiet time — no phone, no scrolling — so you can notice what you actually feel.

## Thought 1

Even if you consider yourself to be a natural submissive, you will find it difficult at times to maintain the level of obedience and service you have consented to give. This is especially true as you first start your journey of self exploration through a service relationship. Submission requires more than just a natural desire — it requires the mental and physical discipline of training. Think of yourself as an artist who must learn the techniques necessary to make your vision a reality.

## Thought 2

I find comfort and reassurance in the knowledge that I will be punished for my mistakes. The punishment I receive and the forgiveness that follows allow me the freedom to be human. It is a demonstration to me that I do not need to be perfect to be loved and valued.
