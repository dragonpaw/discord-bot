## Guidepost: Usefulness

How can I be useful, of what service can I be? There is something inside me, what can it be? — Vincent van Gogh

*In other words: Van Gogh felt he had something valuable to offer the world, and kept searching for what it was and how to use it.*

## Thought 1

Do not confuse an unwillingness to "take" control with a lack of strength or ability to handle Ownership. A good Owner will judiciously exercise control that is freely given; an abuser is one who will "take" it.

## Thought 2

Some submissives act up or misbehave to get their Owner's attention. Even when it works, the attention doesn't last, and it can wear out your Owner's patience. If you need more attention, it's much better to just say so.
