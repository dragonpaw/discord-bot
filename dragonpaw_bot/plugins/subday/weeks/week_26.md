## Guidepost: Motivation

Don't say that you want to give, but go ahead and give! You'll never catch up with a mere hope. — Johann Wolfgang von Goethe

*In other words: don't just talk about wanting to give. Actually do it, because hoping on its own gets you nowhere.*

## Thought 1

Trust and respect your Owner, but remember they're human and will make mistakes. Don't treat them as so perfect that a single mistake destroys your trust in them.

## Thought 2

Submissives often have a hard time saying "no" to others — not just to their Owner. Be aware of the primary commitments you have made so that you do not overextend yourself and end up failing those most important to you.

List five people or things you are grateful to have in your life and explain some of the reasons you value them so much.
