## Guidepost: Fulfillment

It is one of the most beautiful compensations of life, that no man can sincerely try to help another without helping himself. — Ralph Waldo Emerson

*In other words: you can't really try to help someone without it helping you too.*

## Thought 1

Crying is nothing to be ashamed of. Tears often show how much you care.

## Thought 2

Your submission is a powerful gift. Give it to someone who understands how to look after it. Someone who doesn't understand what they've been given can hurt you, even without meaning to.

## Writing Assignment: Accomplishments

Describe something you feel proud to have accomplished. Big or small both count.
