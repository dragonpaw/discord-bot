## Guidepost: Surrender

True surrender is a victory, not a defeat. When you can embrace the unknown and find strength in your vulnerability, then you will know real freedom.

*In other words: giving up control isn't losing. When you can be okay with not knowing what comes next, and feel strong even while you're vulnerable, that's real freedom.*

## Thought 1

A submissive shouldn't go looking for punishment. When you do make a mistake, the way through is to accept the consequence your Owner chooses, be genuinely sorry, and really try not to do it again. That's how you get forgiven and move on. (Punishment only ever happens within limits you've both agreed to.)

## Thought 2

As you explore new areas of physical submission, don't overlook the emotional and spiritual aspects. These areas can provide you with as much fulfillment and satisfaction as you gain from physical acts of submission. As you grow and learn, you will find these areas will become more integrated and very often indistinguishable from each other.
