## Guidepost: Loyalty

Great services are not canceled by one act or by one single error. — Benjamin Disraeli

*In other words: one mistake doesn't erase all the good someone has done.*

## Thought 1

There will be times when you get discouraged or feel resentful of the expectations of your Owner. This is not necessarily an indication of a lack of dedication on your part; more likely it is an indication that you are doing too much too soon or your needs are not being met. Whatever the cause, it is important that you communicate your difficulties to your Owner so the two of you can work out a solution to the problem.

## Thought 2

Only when I allow myself to be vulnerable can I experience the fulfillment that comes from having my trust in another verified.

*In other words: I only get to find out that someone is trustworthy when I let myself be vulnerable with them.*

## Writing Assignment: What You're Into

What's the last story you really got into? It could be a book, a show, a game, a webcomic, a fanfic, a podcast — anything. What drew you to it, and did you enjoy it?
