## Guidepost: Comfort

Give what you have. To someone, it may be better than you dare to think. — Henry Wadsworth Longfellow

*In other words: don't hold back because you think what you have to offer isn't good enough. Someone might value it far more than you'd guess.*

## Thought 1

Being a good submissive requires an extraordinary amount of patience. However, if you focus on trusting your Owner and having faith in yourself, you will find the keys to maintaining that level of patience necessary to fulfilling your commitment to submission.

## Thought 2

Keep learning. Staying curious and building your knowledge makes you a better submissive, and a more interesting person.

## Writing Assignment: Happiness

Describe a moment from the past week when you felt happy. It can be small — a good meal, a funny message, finishing a task. What made it a good moment?
