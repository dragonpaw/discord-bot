## Guidepost: Listen

Hearing is an act of the body. Listening is an act of the heart.

*In other words: hearing just means the sounds reach you. Listening means actually paying attention to what someone means, and caring about it.*

## Thought 2

Owners and submissives often joke about Owners being able to "see all and know all". We all consciously admit that this is not the case, but sometimes submissives will hold a subconscious belief that this is true. While this is a wonderful testament to the respect the submissive has for the Owner, it can be dangerous for the relationship. The communication necessary to maintain the relationship will suffer because the submissive will expect that the Owner already knows whatever it is that needs to be communicated. Worse, the submissive will sometimes resent the Owner for not knowing something that the submissive failed to communicate. So, if there is something your Owner needs to know, speak up. Owners can "know all and see all", but only if their submissives keep them informed.
