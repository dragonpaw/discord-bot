## Guidepost: Passion

Let the things you're passionate about guide you. They're good clues to where you should go next.

## Thought 1

I know a submissive who was told, "You will never make a good submissive because you are too intelligent, too stubborn and too sarcastic." I do not believe those are negative qualities for a submissive to possess. An intelligent submissive is a valuable asset to any Owner, a stubborn submissive will keep going in the face of adversity, and a sarcastic submissive can be a source of amusement for an Owner under the right circumstances.
