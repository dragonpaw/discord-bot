## Guidepost: Inspiration

Small acts of kindness can have big effects you never get to see, the way ripples spread out from a pebble dropped in water.

## Thought 1

Submission requires active participation. Even if you only consent to submit for a specified scene, you are more than just a target for someone's flogger. The more you give, the more you will get back.

## Thought 2

Take compliments as seriously as you take criticism, and accept them with a simple "thank you". Brushing praise off isn't modest. The best way to show you value it is to keep living up to it.
