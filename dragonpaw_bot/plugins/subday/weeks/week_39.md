## Guidepost: Faith

Faith means continuing to trust someone even when it's hard to. It's a kind of strength, and it's worth having.

## Thought 1

Grace in movement comes when you are at ease within your body and your physical surroundings. Grace in action comes when you are at ease within your heart.

*In other words: you move gracefully when you're comfortable in your body and your space, and you act gracefully when you're at peace with yourself.*

## Thought 2

Honor and integrity are the ethical qualities that compel us to behave as if our Owners were right beside us even if they are half a world away.

## Writing Assignment: Your Turn

Write your own assignment this week, then answer it. Pick any topic about yourself, your service, or your dynamic that you'd like to explore. If you're stuck, try one of these:

- Something you wish your Owner asked you about.
- A question from an earlier week you'd answer differently now.
- Something you'd want a brand-new sub to know.
