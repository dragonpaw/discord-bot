1. Each week you get four prompts: a **Guidepost**, two **Thoughts**, and a **Writing assignment**.
   - **Guidepost and Thoughts:** write a few sentences for each. There's no right answer. You can say what it means to you, whether you agree or disagree (disagreeing is completely fine), or give an example from your own life.
   - **Writing assignment:** this one's longer. A few paragraphs is plenty.
   - **No Owner right now?** Answer based on a past dynamic, the one you'd like to have, or how the idea applies to you in general.
   - Write wherever works for you: pen and paper, a Google Doc, a notes app on your phone, anything. Bullet points are fine. Take your time and write when you're ready.
2. Show your work to your Owner. If you aren't Owned, share it with any staff member and talk through your answers together.
