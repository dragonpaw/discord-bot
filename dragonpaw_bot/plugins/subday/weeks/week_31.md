## Guidepost: Excellence

The greatest virtues are those which are most useful to other persons. — Aristotle

## Thought 1

No amount of training makes service real unless you're doing it because you care about the person.

## Thought 2

Take responsibility for your mistakes — even if you are the only one who would ever know about them. A lie by omission (hiding something by just not mentioning it) is still a lie, and demonstrates a lack of faith in your Owner's ability to forgive.
