## Guidepost: Acceptance

Appreciate what you actually have, instead of being sad about what you don't have.

## Thought 1

Most of us did not grow up in a consensual service relationship household so we did not have role models for how to live in a healthy service relationship. Since we do not have observational experience to guide us, it is important to seek out information on real-life service relationships. This also means a service relationship will require more work in the beginning than a traditional relationship, so do not get discouraged when the relationship gets rocky.

## Thought 2

If you don't take other people's commitments seriously — their relationships, their collars, their promises — you make your own commitment to your Owner look less meaningful too.
