## Guidepost: Duty

Duty means doing what you've committed to, fully and every time, even when nobody is checking. You can't do more than your duty, and you shouldn't want to do less.

## Thought 1

There is no such thing as "just" a submissive.

*In other words: being a submissive is never "just" anything. It's a real role that takes skill and effort, and it deserves respect, including from you.*

## Thought 2

Submissives aren't meant to be silent, but sometimes staying quiet is the smart move: you can't say something you regret if you don't say it. Use your judgement, and never stay quiet about your needs, your safety, or your limits.

## Writing Assignment: What Makes You Work Well

Think about the things that are specific to you: your habits, your limits, how you need things communicated. How do those things actually help your dynamic run smoothly day to day? Give real examples if you can.
