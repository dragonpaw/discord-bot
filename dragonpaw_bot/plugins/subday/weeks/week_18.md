## Guidepost: Growth

Change is inevitable. We either grow or we wither. I choose to grow.

## Thought 1

There is a saying that it is easier to get forgiveness than permission. While this may be true, it is not desirable conduct for a submissive. If you know your Owner will not approve of your actions, you need to honor your commitment to abide by your Owner's rules. If you think there is a possibility that your Owner will not approve of something you would like to do, ask first.

## Thought 2

Your Owner has offered you guidance, protection and comfort. If you hide your feelings or do not seek guidance, you are denying your Owner the pleasure that they gain from providing for your well-being. It is the same as if you were denied the pleasure you receive from serving.

## Writing Assignment: Memory Lane

Describe a time when you were younger that you did something for someone else and it felt good — helping out at home, looking after a pet or a sibling, helping a friend. If childhood memories are hard for you, pick any time from your past instead.
