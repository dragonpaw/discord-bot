## Guidepost: Creativity

If you can imagine a pathway, you can find a way to follow it. Sometimes the best reason to do something is simply that no one has ever tried it.

## Thought 1

One of the most valuable services you can do for your Owner is to be an interested listener. An interested listener is someone who pays attention to what is being said and asks questions in order to understand what the other person is saying. Try not to distract your Owner from the main point, but ask for clarification or background information when you start to get confused.

## Thought 2

Self-confidence gives me the ability to relinquish control.

*In other words: I need to feel secure in myself before I can hand control to someone else.*

## Writing Assignment: A Different Perspective

Imagine your bed could talk. What would it say about you? This is a fun way of asking: what are your habits around sleep, rest, comfort, and sex? Write it in your bed's voice if that's fun for you, or just describe those habits directly.
