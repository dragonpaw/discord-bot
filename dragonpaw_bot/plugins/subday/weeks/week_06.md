## Guidepost: Understanding

One of the greatest gifts you can give another person is the gift of understanding. To give this gift you must look beneath the surface of the truth that lies within.

*In other words: understanding someone is one of the best gifts you can give. That means looking past what a person says or does on the surface, and trying to work out what they really feel or mean.*

## Thought 1

A great skill for a submissive is anticipating what your Owner needs or wants before they ask. It isn't magic; you learn it by paying attention. Notice what your Owner enjoys, which topics they get excited about, and what they like in other people. If reading faces or body language is hard for you, that's okay. Asking directly ("Would you like X?") or keeping notes on their preferences works just as well. How could you use what you notice in your service?

## Thought 2

Remember, Ownership is as much a consensual activity as submission. An Owner needs to earn trust and respect before gaining that priceless gift of surrender. A submissive also needs to earn that same level of trust and respect before expecting to have that gift accepted.

## Writing Assignment: Entertainment

Making someone laugh or have a good time counts as service. Describe a time you did that for someone you were serving — a joke, a game you played together, a performance, a meme you sent, anything. If you haven't served anyone yet, describe a time you entertained a friend or partner.
