## Guidepost: Dreams

Go confidently in the direction of your dreams. Live the life you have imagined. — Henry David Thoreau

## Thought 1

There is true freedom in total surrender. Expectations and fears are our only real shackles.

*In other words: when I fully hand over control, the only things still holding me back are my own expectations and fears.*

## Thought 2

A collar is a symbol of the commitment between you and your Owner. It's a physical reminder that your relationship is consensual and that you both still agree to its terms. Not everyone wears a collar all the time, or at all; a day collar, a piece of jewelry, or another symbol can mean the same thing.

## Writing Assignment: Physical Examination

List five things you like about your body or how you look, and say why. They can be anything — your hands, your laugh, your style, how strong you are. If this one feels hard, that's okay; start with one or two and see where it goes.
