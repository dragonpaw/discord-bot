## Guidepost: Learning

It is the people who can do nothing who can find nothing to do, and the secret of happiness in this world is not only to be useful, but to be forever elevating one's usefulness. — Sarah Orne Jewett

*In other words: if you're bored, that's a sign to go learn something new. Happiness comes from being useful, and from always finding ways to be more useful. Taking a cooking class, picking up a craft, or learning a skill your Owner would appreciate all count.*

## Thought 1

Being responsible for another person is a big deal. Knowing that my Owner thinks I'm worth that effort makes me feel humble and grateful. It makes me want to keep getting better at serving, so I'm easier to look after.

## Thought 2

By surrender of my body, my spirit is set free.

*In other words: when I hand control of my body to my Owner, I stop having to decide everything myself, and my mind gets to rest.*

## Writing Assignment: Emotional Fulfillment

What three things do you need for serving to actually feel good to you? (Examples: praise, routine, knowing exactly what's expected, physical affection, feeling noticed.) For each one, explain why you need it.
