## Guidepost: Needs

Honor your needs. They provide the fuel to live your life and follow your passions.

## Thought 1

Try not to fall into the trap of having an "I'm not that kind of submissive" attitude. This attitude is usually a sign that you are uncertain about your ability to succeed in a particular area. If you demonstrate a willingness to try, your efforts will be appreciated whether you are successful or not. (This is about skills and tasks you're unsure of, not your limits. "I'm not that kind of submissive" is a perfectly good answer when it's about a hard limit.)

## Thought 2

Whenever possible, choose joy.

## Writing Assignment: Fantasy Job

If you could have any job in any fictional world — a game, a book, a show, a myth, even Santa's workshop — what would it be and why? What does your choice say about how you like to serve?
