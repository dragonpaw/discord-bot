## Guidepost: Courage

Face your fears, know the risks, and just go for it!

## Thought 1

One person's idea of a precious gift may be another person's idea of an unwanted burden. If the gift is not desirable, it will not be cherished.

*In other words: something that feels like a wonderful gift to you might feel like a chore to someone else. Give your Owner what they actually want, not what you'd want.*

## Thought 2

I have often heard submissives try to justify their rudeness to others by stating, "I am only submissive to one person." Being polite and courteous to someone does not require submission; it merely requires that you be a kind, decent human being. There is also the mistaken belief that it somehow elevates your Owner in the eyes of others if they are the only one to whom you show respect. Your Owner is seen by others to be responsible for your actions; therefore, any offense you give to someone is also given by your Owner. It is not much of an accomplishment for your Owner to own someone that no one else can tolerate.

## Writing Assignment: Real Role Models

Who is a real person (not a fictional character) that you look up to as a submissive? They can be someone you know, someone in your community, or a creator or writer you follow online. What about them do you want to be more like?
