## Guidepost: Dignity

There is a healthful hardiness about real dignity that never dreads contact and communion with others, however humble. — Washington Irving

*In other words: people with real dignity aren't afraid to mix with anyone, however "low" others think that person is. Dignity doesn't come from staying above other people.*

## Thought 1

There will be some skills that you will never fully acquire. Instead of perfection, aim for competence in those cases. We can't all be concert pianists but we can all learn to make music in some form.

## Thought 2

Some people think a submissive has to give up all their personal power. But a powerless submissive can't do much. A submissive who knows their own strengths and chooses to use them under their Owner's direction is far more useful.
